## Summary
This article highlights 25 open-source cybersecurity tools designed to address budget constraints across threat detection, secrets management, compliance, and AI security. These tools cover full lifecycle needs from development to operations, offering automation, visibility, and remediation guidance without enterprise costs.

## Key Points
- Allama enables visual workflow automation for threat detection with 80+ tool integrations.
- Gitleaks/Betterleaks and Cloud-audit focus on secrets scanning and AWS compliance with actionable fixes.
- OpenClaw Scanner and Sage address autonomous AI agent risks via detection and interception.
- pfSense CE and Zabbix provide budget-friendly firewall and monitoring solutions.
- Comp AI automates SOC 2/ISO 27001 compliance, replacing paid vendors like Vanta.
